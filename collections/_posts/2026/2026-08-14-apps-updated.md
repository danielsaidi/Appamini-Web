We have just published new. updated versions of Appamini ABC and Appamini 123 on the App Store, since the old versions were too old for Apple to host.

![Site header](/assets/headers/website.jpg)

## App Updates

We received an email from Apple in June, where they said our apps had to be updated to stay on the App Store.

Since we haven't updated the apps in some time, we decided that we we're going to try updating them, and just remove them in case updating them involved too much work.

To our big delight, we just had to update the deployment targets to iOS 15 and rebuild the apps with the latest version of Xcode (the tool you use top build apps for Apple's platforms).

We are therefore happy to announce that the apps you download from the App Store are now build for modern platform versions, and ready in case we want to take them further.
