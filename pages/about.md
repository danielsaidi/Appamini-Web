Appamini is a small team that builds educational apps for kids. Through slow play, we hope to make learning fun.

## Our philosophy

We at Appamini love apps, and truly believe that the format is a game changer when it comes to making children learn through games. 

Since the world of apps has changed drastically since we first launched, we think it's important to underline that you can always count on us to honor these principles:

* We don't like apps that are translated in the App Store, when the app is in one language. We therefore tailor our apps for each supported language.
* We don't like in-app purchases and subscriptions in apps targeted at children. We therefore have no in-app purchases or subscriptions in our apps.
* We don't like ads and sponsored content in apps targeted at children. We therefore have no ads in our apps.
* We don't like "pink for girls and blue for boys". Therefore, we make our apps equally colorful for all genders.


## Our history

What happens when a software developer on parental leave and a daughter that is crazy about the alphabet put their heads together? An app about the alphabet, of course.

It did not take long for 5-year-old Cornelia and her father Daniel to engage the rest of the family and a bunch of friends. In autumn 2012, after three months work, Swedish *Alphabet Games* was released.

The app received a decent number of downloads and was ported to an English version, but when the family met illustrator Laura Di Francesco, it became clear that it was time to take the next step. 

Alphabet Games was redesigned from the ground up with Laura’s amazing illustrations, and the alphabet village was beginning to take shape. 

After a couple of months, the new app Appamini ABC was finished and the company was Appamini founded. It was followed by Appamini 123, which expanded the world to number city.


## Today

We are currently not developing our apps, but keep them up to date and make sure they work on new devices and os versions.
